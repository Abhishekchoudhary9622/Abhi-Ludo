# Game Modes (Classic vs Quick Mode)

Pip Rally implements standard production Ludo King game modes: **Classic Mode** and **Quick Mode**.

---

## 1. Classic Mode

- **Duration:** 15–35 minutes.
- **Starting Tokens:** All 4 tokens begin in the yard (base).
- **Target:** All 4 tokens must reach the final home triangle (progress 57).
- **Home Entrance:** Open freely without requiring a prior kill.
- **Pacing:** Strategic, deliberate racing across the 52-cell track.

---

## 2. Quick Mode

- **Duration:** 5–10 minutes.
- **Starting Tokens:** **2 tokens start deployed on the board** at progress 0 (entry cell); 2 tokens remain in base.
- **Target:** First player to bring **1 token** to the final home triangle wins the match.
- **Home Corridor Unlock:** A player **MUST capture (kill) at least 1 opponent token** during the match before any of their tokens can enter the home lane (progress 51..57).
- **Pacing:** High-tempo, aggressive capture-focused gameplay designed for rapid sessions.

---

## 3. Shared Mechanics Across Modes

- **6 to Leave Base:** Bringing a token out of the yard requires rolling a 6.
- **Bonus Rolls:** Granted on rolling a 6, capturing an opponent token, or finishing a token into the home triangle.
- **Tactical Chaining:** Kills and home arrivals reset the consecutive-6 counter, enabling dynamic turn extensions.
- **3rd Six Cap:** Third consecutive 6 in a single turn chain is constrained to 1..5 in the RNG.
- **Safe Cells:** 8 safe cells (4 starting arrows + 4 star/pip-flower cells) provide full immunity from capture.
- **Blocking:** 2 or more of the same opponent's tokens stacked on a non-safe cell blocks that cell completely.
